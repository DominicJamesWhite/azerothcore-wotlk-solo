Talent trees as this server actually runs them — not retail 3.3.5a.

Talent point rules and prerequisite-arrow geometry adapted from wowsims/wotlk (MIT). Tooltips are resolved from this server's own DBCs, because Wowhead has no knowledge of custom talents.

Two tooltip constructs are choices rather than resolutions: conditional text ($?s) always shows its first branch, and gendered text always shows the male form. Values that depend on your character (attack power, spell power, weapon damage) are left as raw markup.
